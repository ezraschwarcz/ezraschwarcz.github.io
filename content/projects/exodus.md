## The work

Announcements only get one first impression. For each of these I wrote the post and made the media that went with it, so the news landed in a single screen: what is new, why it matters, and where to go next.

The posts link out to X, where you can see each one as it ran.

## What I do for clients

- **Launch announcements.** The post, the video or artwork, and the timing, planned around the product going live.
- **Content calendars.** Weeks of posts planned ahead, so the account never goes quiet between launches. For Tohu Movies I planned a 90-day calendar of daily posts with matching artwork.
- **Artwork for every platform.** Profile pictures, banners and post images sized correctly for each network.
- **Reporting.** A plain summary of what worked and what to do more of.
